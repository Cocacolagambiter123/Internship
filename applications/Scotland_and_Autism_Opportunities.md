# Scotland-only and autism-focused opportunities

Compiled 6 October 2026. You graduate in June 2027, so check each item's year-of-study rule: some accept final-year students and recent graduates, others only take students returning to university.

## Adjustments on mainstream applications
Under the Equality Act 2010 you can ask for adjustments, such as extra time on online tests, without naming your condition. Use each firm's own adjustments form. EmployAbility can contact firms for you.
